How a send is checked
Said-No · User flow
01
Connect
Your own Sui wallet, on testnet or mainnet. The server holds no spending key.
02
Sign in
Sign a message once. It moves no funds. It unlocks your memories only.
03
Enter a send
Recipient and amount. Nothing is sent yet.
04
Gather evidence
Tatum gRPC: balance, your history, dry run.
Walrus Memory: your past refusals, recalled.
05
The engine decides
NORMALREMINDERREFLECTIONHOLD
Fixed weights: recipient .35, amount .30, hour .20, history .15. If Tatum or Memory fails, the answer is at least Reflection. Your own reason is quoted back, with the counterfactual.
Hold needs a "never this address" memory, or two matching refusals at 0.85 or higher. Approvals count too: approve more than you refused, and the friction drops.
06A · Deny
Say why
Type your reason. It is saved to Walrus Memory in about 25 seconds, and the blob ID is your receipt.
06B · Proceed
Wallet signs, Tatum broadcasts
Server builds the transfer, your wallet signs it, Tatum sends it. Your approval is remembered too.
↺ The next time a similar send comes up, step 04 recalls what you saved in step 06.
